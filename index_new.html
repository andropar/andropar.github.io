01 / About

I'm an ML engineer combining production experience with research expertise in large-scale vision systems. Currently finishing a PhD (expected 2026) at the Max Planck Institute while building ML systems that scale.

I've built and deployed production ML systems processing 20M+ images at CHECK24, engineered data pipelines handling billions of images (LAION-2B → 120M curated dataset), and developed neural network solutions that improved accuracy from 50% to 92% at Webdata Solutions.

My work spans the full ML lifecycle: data engineering, model development, production deployment, and performance optimization. I'm looking for ML engineering or applied research roles where I can build impactful systems at scale.
