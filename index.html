Hi, I’m Johannes

I study how brains see things, with a soft spot for good data, clear code, and gentle explanations.

I’m a PhD student in computational cognitive neuroscience at the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences in Leipzig, working with Martin Hebart. My work focuses on making deep phenotyping studies in vision more efficient – from faster fMRI acquisition to smarter stimulus selection.
